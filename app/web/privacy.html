Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

BrewQ lets you order drinks at a single coffee shop from the BrewQ Android app and pick them up at the counter. This page explains what the app, the barista panel and our server store about you. It is short because there is little to tell.

What we store on our server

  • A device identifier. The app generates a random ID (a UUID) the first time it starts. We use it to recognise your phone, because there are no accounts and no sign-in. It carries no information about you. If you clear the app's data or reinstall it, the app creates a new ID and starts without your history.
  • Your name, if you enter one. It is optional, up to 40 characters, and is shown to the barista on your order.
  • Your cart: the drinks, sizes and quantities you picked.
  • Your orders: the pickup code, items, sizes, quantities, the prices at the time of the order, the total in AMD, the order status and the times it changed.

We do not collect your email, phone number, address, date of birth or payment details. You pay at the counter when you pick the order up.

Your location

When your order is ready, the app can show a map with the coffee shop and, if you allow it, your position. Your position is used only on your phone, only while the app is open, and is never sent to our server or to anyone else. Allowing location is optional; the map and the route button work without it.

Third parties

  • Map tiles. The map picture is loaded from OpenFreeMap (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors). To deliver the tiles, their servers necessarily see your IP address and which part of the map is requested. The app does not send them your device ID or your name.
  • The "Route" button opens a navigation app of your choice with the coffee shop's coordinates. Nothing about you is passed on. What that app does is covered by its own policy.

Server logs

Like any web server, ours keeps technical logs of requests (time, IP address, the requested address) to keep the service running and to deal with abuse. Retention: TODO(user): number of days.

Cookies, analytics and ads

The app and the public pages use no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. The barista panel is for staff only: it keeps a sign-in token and the chosen interface language in the browser's local storage on the staff device, and nothing else.

Who sees your data

The baristas see your pickup code, your name (if given), the items and the status of your orders in the panel. They do not see your device ID. We do not sell or share your data.
